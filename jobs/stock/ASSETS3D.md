(659 entries, 1.84 GB). Zips on Drive: `TheIfMachine_Media/_assets3d/<group>/<group>.zip`.

Claude pulls with `python3 tools/pp_drive.py get` from `TheIfMachine_Media/_assets3d/`.

use MPFB's built-in **"mixamo" rig, NOT the "game_engine" rig** named in the task file.

## How to use (three.js r160)

- HDRIs: `RGBELoader` / `PMREMGenerator` for IBL. 1k for previews, 2k for finals.
- Materials: PBR maps per folder; `map` = Color/Diffuse, `normalMap` = NormalGL,
  `roughnessMap` = Roughness, `aoMap` = AO, `displacementMap` = Displacement.
- Models/people: `GLTFLoader`; people share the `mixamorig:*` skeleton, so the
  Quaternius AND Mixamo animation .glbs apply to any of the 16 people (same bone names).
- Effects: caustics as an animated light-map sequence; smoke/fire atlases are 4x4
  flipbooks (frame order row-major); bubbles/dust/rain/snow as sprites.
