# Chain — Trái Đất từng đóng băng (ice-ages) · CINEMA §0 · ★ = pure invention
1. an ice cube in a glass of water → (it clinks, slowly melts) → a familiar thing
2. ★ the melting cube → (the camera dives into it: a tiny bubble trapped inside) → a bubble of air
3. the bubble → (it rises out of the glass and floats over a green Swiss meadow) → the meadow
4. the meadow → (a house-sized boulder lies in it, alone, foreign) → an erratic
5. ★ the boulder → (it shrugs off moss and walks back toward the mountains like a lost giant) → where did it come from?
6. the walking boulder → (it stops; a medieval villager points: the Flood!) → the old explanation
7. the Flood → (a woodcut wave washes over the valley, carrying boulders) → Noah's waters
8. ★ the woodcut wave → (it freezes, turns to blue-white ice, the boulders frozen in it) → the other explanation
9. the ice → (a Swiss chamois hunter points at polished, scratched bedrock) → the evidence
10. the scratches → (they line up like the marks of a giant's fingernails) → glaciers dragged stones
11. ★ the fingernail marks → (the camera pulls up: the scratches run across the whole Jura, far beyond any glacier today) → the ice was huge
12. the Jura → (Schimper writes a word in a poem: "Eiszeit") → the word ice age, 1837
13. the word → (it is lifted from his page and placed in Agassiz's lecture) → Neuchâtel, July 1837
14. the lecture → (Agassiz's arm sweeps; ice fills Europe on the map behind him) → the great ice age
15. ★ the audience → (their frowns freeze into icicles hanging from their brows) → scepticism
16. the frozen hall → (thaws; Agassiz in America, the same scratches on New England rock) → the ice was everywhere
17. the rocks → (the ice came and went — several times, layers of moraines) → many ice ages
18. ★ the layers → (they become the rings of a tree, and the rings become orbits around the Sun) → why would ice come and go?
19. the orbits → (a Serbian engineer at a desk with a pencil, 1920s) → Milanković
20. his pencil → (the Earth on its axis wobbles like a spinning top) → precession
21. ★ the top → (its tilt nods; its path stretches into an egg) → the three cycles
22. the egg-shaped orbit → (sunlight on the north rises and falls like a slow tide) → the sunlight schedule
23. the tide → (a calendar of 100,000-year pages turns) → the ice calendar
24. ★ the calendar → (it's printed on ice; a drill bites into it in Antarctica) → Dome C
25. the drill → (it pulls up a core 3 km long, laid out on tables like a road) → 800,000 years of ice
26. the core → (a slice held to the light: tiny bubbles) → ancient air
27. ★ a bubble → (it opens and the air inside is a landscape — mammoths on a cold steppe) → breathing the past
28. the steppe → (the air's CO₂ is measured: a line rising and falling with temperature) → eight cycles
29. the line → (it becomes a mountain range drawn across eight peaks) → the rhythm of the ice
30. the eighth peak → (at its end the line shoots up off the page) → today's CO₂
31. ★ the line off the page → (it becomes the chimney smoke of a modern city) → us
32. the smoke → (it drifts over a glacier that is retreating, leaving a boulder behind) → glaciers today
33. the boulder → (the same kind of erratic as in the opening, freshly left) → the rhyme
34. the boulder → (a child climbs on it) → the next generation
35. ★ the child's hand on the rock → (it fits the ancient scratches like a hand in a handprint) → knowing
36. the scratches → (they glow briefly gold) → understanding
37. the meadow → (the ice cube of the opening is just water in the glass now) → bookend
38. the glass → (a bubble rises and pops) → the last image
39. the pop → (fade to paper) → end
40. (alt bookend) the bubble → (it carries the 800,000-year-old air into the child's breath) → the meaning
