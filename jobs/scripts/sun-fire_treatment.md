# Treatment — Mặt Trời cháy bằng gì? (sun-fire) · director: Claude · 17 Nov 2026
(CINEMA.md §1; the chain: jobs/scripts/sun-fire_chain.md; research: jobs/research/sun-fire.md)

## 1. Logline
Coal would have burnt out in a few thousand years, gravity in a few million — the answer hid in a missing sliver of mass, and its proof in ghost particles that a man waited thirty years to catch at the bottom of a gold mine.

## 1b. The opening (three candidates, STORY §1b patterns — keep one)
- A: Familiar made strange: a match and the Sun side by side — one dies in seconds, the other has burned for billions of years.
- B: The moment before: Eddington in Cardiff, 1920, about to call the old theory a corpse.
- C: The impossible number: every second the Sun turns about four million tonnes of itself into light.
- chosen, and why: Familiar made strange (A).

## 5. Set pieces
### Set piece 1 — The coal Sun (act I)
- idea: the obvious answer fails spectacularly
- staging: the Sun lifted in iron tongs over a Victorian scuttle
- camera: slow push as the hourglass runs
- transformation: the Sun burns to cinders in five thousand years
- sound moment: the crackle of coal, then a hollow silence
- reveal: coal is not enough
### Set piece 2 — The missing bead (act II)
- idea: a sliver of missing mass is the secret of the Sun
- staging: Aston's balance as four and one balloons
- camera: insert on the balance pan → detail on the falling bead
- transformation: the bead flares into E = mc² written in sparks
- sound moment: a tiny tick as it drops, then a swell
- reveal: mass becomes energy
### Set piece 3 — Ghosts in the gold mine (act III)
- idea: the proof came from particles that pass through everything
- staging: the Homestake mine, a tank the size of a house
- camera: drop down the shaft, then the thirty-year wall
- transformation: one atom changes; the chalk prediction ages on the wall; the neutrino changes coat
- sound moment: drips in the mine, then the SNO count matching
- reveal: the Sun's fusion confirmed
