# Chain — Các vì sao xa đến đâu (star-distance) · CINEMA §0 · ★ = pure invention
1. your thumb held up at arm's length against a window → (blink one eye, then the other: the thumb JUMPS against the street — parallax lives in your own face) → the thumb jumps
2. ★ the jumping thumb → (the thumb's nail swells into a pale disc, the street behind darkens into night sky) → the Moon
3. the Moon → (the camera rises past it; the black fills with pinpricks) → a sky of stars that never move
4. a sky of stars → (the stars are pinned like insects on a velvet board in a cabinet — "fixed stars") → a collector's star cabinet
5. ★ the star cabinet → (one pin trembles; the glass of the cabinet becomes the lens of a telescope) → a brass telescope in Königsberg
6. the telescope → (we slide down the tube into the eyepiece's circle) → the eyepiece's view: two stars, one faint, one near
7. the eyepiece view → (the circle splits into two circles: January and July — the Earth's two ends of its orbit) → two views side by side
8. ★ two views → (they are laid over each other like tracing paper: the near star has slid a hair's width) → the shift drawn as an ink tick
9. the ink tick → (the tick is so small it becomes a coin seen from a city away: 13 km, a 2-cm coin) → a coin on a far hill
10. the coin on a hill → (the hill tilts up into a triangle whose base is the Earth's orbit) → the parallax triangle drawn in ink
11. the triangle → (its long thin point stretches out and out, faster than the camera can follow) → 61 Cygni at its tip
12. 61 Cygni → (its light becomes a thread unspooling from a bobbin for ten years) → a thread ten light-years long
13. ★ the thread → (it pulls taut and becomes the line of a page in Bessel's 1838 paper) → Bessel's printed number, circled in red
14. the circled number → (the page folds into a paper boat and sails off into the dark) → most stars are too far for triangles
15. the paper boat → (it sinks into a sea of a thousand thousand stars) → the Milky Way
16. the Milky Way → (a glass photographic plate slides in front of it, the sky captured as black dots on white) → a Harvard plate
17. the plate → (we pull back: a room of women at desks, each with a plate and a loupe) → the Harvard "computers"
18. the computers' room → (one desk comes forward; a loupe's circle fills the frame) → Henrietta Leavitt's loupe
19. ★ the loupe → (dots in the circle swell and shrink in rhythm, like breathing) → blinking Cepheid stars
20. breathing stars → (each breath leaves a mark on a strip, the strip becomes a graph) → period against brightness
21. the graph → (the points fall into a straight line, and the line becomes a ruler) → a ruler made of light
22. ★ the ruler of light → (it becomes a candle; the candle is carried further and further away, dimming exactly as the rule says) → the standard candle
23. the receding candle → (it shrinks into one of the spiral smudges in the sky) → a spiral nebula
24. the spiral nebula → (it swirls into a question mark) → the question: inside our galaxy or another universe?
25. the question mark → (it splits into two lecterns facing each other in a hall) → the Great Debate, 1920
26. the two lecterns → (the hall's chandelier becomes the dome of Mount Wilson opening) → the 100-inch telescope
27. the dome → (a glass plate slides out of the telescope) → plate H335H of Andromeda
28. ★ plate H335H → (an "N" in Hubble's hand is struck through; "VAR!" is written — the ink still wet, and the red exclamation mark glows) → the word VAR!
29. VAR! → (the exclamation point becomes the blinking star in Andromeda, breathing like Leavitt's) → a Cepheid in Andromeda
30. the Andromeda Cepheid → (Leavitt's light-ruler is laid against it and the ruler grows a mile, a thousand miles, a million light-years) → the distance to Andromeda
31. ★ the ruler running out → (the Milky Way, which filled the sky, shrinks to one swirl among thousands, like a raindrop in a storm) → a universe of galaxies
32. the galaxies → (their colours shift to red, as a train's whistle drops as it leaves) → redshifted galaxies
33. red galaxies → (they all move apart like raisins in a rising loaf) → the expanding universe
34. ★ the rising loaf → (the loaf is the oven's dark; the oven door becomes a priest's study, Lemaître at his desk) → Lemaître, 1927
35. Lemaître's page → (his equation's line becomes Hubble's straight line of 1929) → Hubble's graph
36. Hubble's graph → (the line extends backwards to a single point) → the beginning of everything (hint, not stated)
37. the single point → (it opens like an eye — the Gaia telescope's mirror) → Gaia in space
38. ★ Gaia → (a billion tiny ink ticks fill the sky at once, each one a Bessel measurement) → a billion parallaxes
39. a billion ticks → (they become a three-dimensional map that the camera flies through) → the Milky Way in depth
40. the map → (we fly out of it, back through the atmosphere to a window) → the window of the opening
41. ★ the window → (a thumb rises against it; one eye blinks; the thumb jumps — and now we know how far the stars are) → the opening, changed
42. the thumb → (the camera pulls back to a child at the window, looking up) → the last image: the question passed on
