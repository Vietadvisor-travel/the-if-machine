# Director's script — Nhìn xuyên bóng tối (see-in-the-dark)

Written FIRST (owner, 4 Nov 2026). The look: the gravity-half v2 standard, pushed further (owner, 11 Nov 2026: "more premium,
more visual and creative; no overlapping elements or captions"). A real 2D cartoon: every shot designed on its own, an in-scene
camera rig (top-down, low angle, close-ups, extreme wides, cuts), action on every line. This episode's own device: the viewer
LOOKS THROUGH each kind of eye (the cat's silver view, your dark-adapted view, green goggles, the snake's heat-sight) with real
darkness masks, light cones and thermal silhouettes. Layout law: titles 6–24 %, subjects 26–70 %, a calm caption band 72–81 %
that no subject or label may enter; labels are placed by a collision-avoiding sign system. One vertical 9:16 film, about 7 min,
af_heart at --speed 1.0. No quotations, no legend. Theme: noir.

REAL: Britain, Second World War, Vitamin A

## Spine
- Hook: no moon, no lamp, no light at all, and you see everything → the question → a pit viper finds a mouse's warmth from a
  metre away in total darkness → the promise: how a cat stretches the light, what your eyes can secretly do, what it takes when
  the light is gone.
- 1) The cat upgrade: one sixth of the light → the pupil (135× against 15×) → the mirror (tapetum) → rods 6–8× denser → the
  price: blurry, less colour, and nothing at all with zero light.
- 2) What you already have: 20–30 minutes of dark adaptation → ×100,000 → a single particle of light (2016) → a phone screen
  resets it → red lamps keep it → what people say: carrots (the wartime story).
- 3) Borrowed eyes: goggles multiply light tens of thousands of times → still nothing in a sealed cave → 2019: mice that see
  infrared → but that is still lamp light; true dark needs the glow of heat.
- 4) Seeing heat: the pits → a few thousandths of a degree, the wasabi sensor → one picture in the brain → a map of warmth →
  no colour, no windows → warm eyes would glow.
- Conclusion: stretch the light · your eyes already adapt · true dark needs heat → not brighter eyes but a different kind of
  light; the dark was never empty → tonight: lamp off, phone down, thirty minutes → next: breathe underwater.
