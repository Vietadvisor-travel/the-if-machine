# Treatment — Các vì sao xa đến đâu (star-distance) · director: Claude · 17 Nov 2026
(CINEMA.md §1; the chain: jobs/scripts/star-distance_chain.md; research: jobs/research/star-distance.md)

## 1. Logline
A blink of your own eyes, a German astronomer's hair-width measurement, a deaf woman's blinking stars and one word scrawled on a glass plate — and the universe turned out to be millions of times bigger than anyone dared to think.

## 1b. The opening (three candidates, STORY §1b patterns — keep one)
- A: Familiar made strange: your thumb jumps when you blink — that jump measures the stars.
- B: The moment before: Bessel at the eyepiece in Königsberg, 1838, cold hands, a star that will not keep still.
- C: The impossible number: the nearest star's light left it before you were born — how could anyone know that?
- chosen, and why: Familiar made strange (A) — it puts the method in the viewer's own face in the first five seconds.

## 2. The feeling
- word: vertigo of scale
- the moment it lands: L31, the Milky Way shrinking to one swirl among thousands

## 3. Governing image & motif
- image: the parallax blink — one point that jumps against the background
- motif (shape): the CIRCLE of the lens: the eye, the eyepiece, the loupe, the telescope dome, Gaia's mirror
- returns (≥ 4): L1 — the eye blinks · L6 — the eyepiece · L18 — Leavitt's loupe · L26 — the dome opens · L37 — Gaia's mirror

## 4. Visual metaphors
- distance too big to imagine: (1) a coin seen from 13 km → (2) a thread of light unspooling for ten years → (3) the Milky Way as one raindrop in a storm of galaxies

## 5. Set pieces
### Set piece 1 — The jumping thumb (act I)
- idea: parallax in your own face becomes the stars' parallax
- staging: a window at night, the thumb, the street, the sky
- camera: close on the eye → pull back through the window to the sky
- transformation: the thumb's nail becomes the Moon becomes the stars; the two views overlay
- sound moment: a soft click of the blink, then silence
- reveal: the near star has slid a hair's width — measurable
### Set piece 2 — VAR! (act II)
- idea: one stroke of a pen moves the edge of the universe
- staging: Mount Wilson dome at night, the plate on a light table
- camera: insert on the plate → detail on the N → push into the star
- transformation: N struck through, VAR! written; Leavitt's ruler laid against Andromeda
- sound moment: the scratch of the pen, music drops out
- reveal: Andromeda is outside our galaxy
### Set piece 3 — The shrinking Milky Way (act III)
- idea: our whole galaxy becomes one among countless
- staging: the sky as an engraved star chart
- camera: wide pull-back faster and faster
- transformation: the Milky Way shrinks to a swirl, swirls redden and drift apart like raisins
- sound moment: a single low note swelling
- reveal: the universe is expanding

## 6. Hero & acting
