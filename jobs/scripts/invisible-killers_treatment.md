# Treatment — Kẻ giết người vô hình (invisible-killers) · director: Claude · 17 Nov 2026
(CINEMA.md §1; the chain: jobs/scripts/invisible-killers_chain.md; research: jobs/research/invisible-killers.md)

## 1. Logline
In 1840s Vienna one maternity ward killed ten times more mothers than the one next door; a young doctor found why, was laughed at and died in an asylum — and a flask of clear broth and a photograph of a rod-shaped creature finally proved him right.

## 1b. The opening (three candidates, STORY §1b patterns — keep one)
- A: Familiar made strange: a clean-looking hand is a crowded landscape of living things.
- B: The moment before: Semmelweis at night with two columns of numbers he cannot explain.
- C: The wrong answer everyone believed: disease came from bad air — miasma.
- chosen, and why: Familiar made strange (A) — the viewer's own hand.

## 4. Visual metaphors

## 5. Set pieces
### Set piece 1 — Two doors (act I)
- idea: the same hospital, two death rates
- staging: the corridor of the Vienna General Hospital, two doors
- camera: wide corridor → push between the doors
- transformation: the doors become sand-glasses, one running red
- sound moment: footsteps, a bell, then silence
- reveal: ten times more deaths behind one door
### Set piece 2 — The smudges (act II)
- idea: death carried on clean-looking hands
- staging: the autopsy room to the ward
- camera: following the hands as they walk
- transformation: smudges like footprints dissolve in the chlorine basin
- sound moment: a scalpel's clink, then running water
- reveal: handwashing saves mothers
### Set piece 3 — The flask that stayed clear (act III)
- idea: life comes only from life
- staging: Pasteur's lab, the swan-neck flask
- camera: insert on the bend of the neck → detail on the dust settling
- transformation: the calendar turns to today: still clear; snap — the storm
- sound moment: the snap of glass
- reveal: germs come from the air, not from nothing
