# Chain — Mặt Trời cháy bằng gì? (sun-fire) · CINEMA §0 · ★ = pure invention
1. a match struck in a dark room → (its flame steadies; the camera pulls back) → a candle on a sill at dawn
2. the candle → (the window behind it fills with the rising Sun, the two flames side by side) → candle and Sun
3. ★ candle and Sun → (the candle burns down in seconds, the Sun does not move) → the question: what never burns out?
4. the Sun → (it is lifted like a lump of coal in iron tongs) → a coal Sun
5. ★ the coal Sun → (it burns: an hourglass beside it runs out in five thousand years; the Sun goes black as cinders) → coal is not enough
6. the cinder Sun → (it crumbles into a heap of coal in a Victorian scuttle) → a Victorian study
7. the study → (Kelvin at his desk squeezes a rubber ball in his fist; it grows warm) → the shrinking Sun idea
8. the squeezed ball → (it becomes the Sun slowly shrinking, glowing from the squeeze) → gravitational contraction
9. ★ the shrinking Sun → (a measuring tape around its waist ticks tighter year by year) → the Sun's "diet"
10. the tape → (it becomes a calendar of only 20 million pages) → Kelvin's age of the Sun
11. the calendar → (a geologist's hammer cracks a cliff open: layers upon layers) → the rocks' calendar, far longer
12. the cliff layers → (they rise up into a page of Darwin's notebook, a branching tree) → Darwin needs more time
13. ★ Darwin's tree → (the tree's branches grow faster than Kelvin's calendar can turn) → physics and life at war
14. the war → (two scales: the Sun's age on one pan, Earth's on the other — the pans won't balance) → a contradiction
15. the scales → (the pans become two helium balloons and four hydrogen balloons) → Aston's weighing, 1920
16. ★ four hydrogen balloons → (they are tied into one helium balloon — and a tiny bead of mass drops to the floor) → the missing 1 part in 120
17. the dropped bead → (it flares into light: E = mc² written in sparks) → mass becomes energy
18. E = mc² → (the sparks spell out across a lecture hall in Cardiff) → Eddington's address, 1920
19. Eddington → (he lifts a sheet off a coffin: "the contraction theory… an unburied corpse") → the old idea buried
20. the coffin lid closes → (the lid becomes a heavy door into the Sun's core) → the core of the Sun
21. ★ the core → (we shrink to the size of a proton; protons jostle like dancers who repel) → the crowded core
22. the dancers → (two leap too close and fuse — a flash) → fusion
23. the flash → (it becomes a chain of dancers changing partners: the proton–proton chain) → Bethe's chain, 1938–39
24. Bethe's chain → (written as a paper strip feeding into a typewriter) → Bethe's paper
25. ★ the paper strip → (one tiny letter slips off the page and flies straight out of the Sun, through everything) → a neutrino
26. the neutrino → (it passes through the Sun's skin, through space, through the Earth like light through glass) → a ghost particle
27. the ghost → (it goes through a man walking down a street, through his coffee, unnoticed) → billions through your thumbnail every second
28. ★ the thumbnail → (it becomes the opening of a mine shaft; we drop down 1,478 m) → the Homestake gold mine
29. the mine → (a tank the size of a house full of cleaning fluid) → Ray Davis's tank
30. the tank → (one atom in it changes colour, like one grain of sand turned gold) → a caught neutrino
31. the gold grain → (a counter ticks: one… two… far fewer than the chalk prediction on the wall) → only about a third
32. ★ the chalk prediction → (it hangs in the air for thirty years as the wall ages around it, posters change, hair turns grey) → the solar neutrino problem
33. the ageing wall → (it cracks open into a cavern in Canada; a sphere of heavy water) → the Sudbury observatory
34. the sphere → (three doors in it: three kinds of neutrino) → three flavours
35. ★ three doors → (a neutrino walks in one door in a red coat and out another in a blue coat) → oscillation: they changed on the way
36. the coat change → (the count on the wall finally matches the chalk) → the Sun confirmed
37. the matching count → (the numbers glow and rise up out of the mine to the dawn sky) → the Sun again
38. ★ the Sun → (we see it for what it is: a sphere where every second millions of tonnes of matter become light) → the fusion furnace
39. the furnace → (its light falls on a field of wheat, a solar panel, a face) → everything we live on
40. the face → (the dawn light in the window of the opening; the candle from the start is out, the Sun is not) → the bookend
41. the window → (the camera rests on the warm light on the sill) → the last image
