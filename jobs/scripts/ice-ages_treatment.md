# Treatment — Trái Đất từng đóng băng (ice-ages) · director: Claude · 17 Nov 2026
(CINEMA.md §1; the chain: jobs/scripts/ice-ages_chain.md; research: jobs/research/ice-ages.md)

## 1. Logline
Giant boulders lay in green fields where no river could have carried them; a quarrelling group of Swiss naturalists saw ice where others saw Noah's flood, and a drill in Antarctica now lets us breathe the air of 800,000 years ago.

## 1b. The opening (three candidates, STORY §1b patterns — keep one)
- A: Familiar made strange: an ice cube in a glass holds a bubble of air — and some ice holds air older than mankind.
- B: The moment before: Agassiz walking onto the stage at Neuchâtel, 1837.
- C: The wrong answer everyone believed: the boulders were dropped by Noah's flood.
- chosen, and why: Familiar made strange (A).

## 5. Set pieces
### Set piece 1 — The walking boulder (act I)
- idea: the rocks are in the wrong place
- staging: a Swiss meadow with an erratic
- camera: wide meadow → the boulder walks to the mountains
- transformation: the woodcut Flood freezes into ice carrying the boulders
- sound moment: cowbells, then the creak of ice
- reveal: glaciers once reached here
### Set piece 2 — The ice calendar (act II)
- idea: the sky sets the timetable of the ice
- staging: Milanković at his desk; the Earth as a spinning top
- camera: detail on the pencil → wide on the orbit
- transformation: the top's wobble and the egg orbit draw the 100,000-year pages
- sound moment: a slow tick like a great clock
- reveal: the cycles
### Set piece 3 — Breathing the past (act III)
- idea: we can hold air older than mankind
- staging: the Antarctic drill camp; the core on tables
- camera: insert on a bubble → into it
- transformation: the bubble opens on a mammoth steppe
- sound moment: wind of the steppe, then silence
- reveal: 800,000 years of climate
