# Chain — Kẻ giết người vô hình (invisible-killers) · CINEMA §0 · ★ = pure invention
1. a hand reaching for a door handle → (dust motes in a sunbeam drift onto the hand) → a clean-looking hand
2. ★ the clean hand → (we dive into the skin: a landscape of ridges where tiny creatures graze like cattle) → a living hand
3. the creatures → (the camera rises out; the hand is in 1846 Vienna, a doctor's sleeve) → Vienna General Hospital
4. the hospital → (a corridor splits into two doors, two wards) → the First and Second Clinic
5. ★ two doors → (each door becomes a sand-glass; in the First the sand runs red, ten times faster) → the death rates
6. the red sand → (it falls into mothers' beds; the curtains draw) → childbed fever
7. the curtains → (rumours on the street drawn as whispers that become a map of women pleading to go to the Second ward) → fear of the First
8. the map → (Semmelweis's notebook: two columns of numbers he cannot explain) → the young doctor's puzzle
9. ★ the two columns → (they become the two halves of a priest's bell that rang as the priest walked through the First ward — he changed the route; nothing changed) → the false clues
10. the bell → (it falls silent; a scalpel on a tray) → the autopsy room
11. the scalpel → (it slips: a red line on a finger) → Kolletschka's cut
12. ★ the red line → (it spreads across the page of his autopsy report like ink in water, and the same pattern spreads across a mother's chart) → the same disease
13. the two pages → (Semmelweis lays one over the other; they match) → the insight: something on the hands
14. the hands → (they walk from the autopsy table along the corridor to the First ward, leaving faint smudges like footprints) → cadaver particles carried
15. ★ the smudges → (a basin of chlorinated lime; the smudges dissolve in it) → handwashing, May 1847
16. the basin → (the red sand-glass slows to a trickle) → deaths fall to one or two in a hundred
17. the slow glass → (it's smashed by a fist on a lecture table) → colleagues refuse
18. the shards → (they become the bars of a window in an asylum) → his end, 1865
19. ★ the barred window → (light through it falls across the sea to Paris, onto a glass flask) → Pasteur's lab
20. the flask → (its neck is heated and bent like a swan's) → the swan-neck flask
21. the swan neck → (dust drifts in on the air and settles in the bend like snow in a valley) → the trap
22. ★ the broth → (it stays clear while a calendar beside it turns: days, weeks — and a modern label: still clear today) → no spontaneous life
23. the clear broth → (the neck snaps; within days the broth clouds like a storm) → life comes from life
24. the storm in the flask → (it swirls into a microscope's circle) → Koch's microscope, 1876
25. the circle → (rod-shaped creatures in it, lined up like matchsticks) → the anthrax bacillus
26. ★ the matchsticks → (they march across a map of a farm, a dead sheep, a cow) → one microbe, one disease
27. Koch's glass slide → (it becomes a photographic plate, the first photograph of a killer) → the photograph
28. the photograph → (pinned on a wall of wanted posters: cholera, tuberculosis 1882) → a rogues' gallery
29. ★ the wanted posters → (each villain's face is a microbe, drawn like an engraved criminal portrait) → the killers named
30. the gallery → (the camera turns: a surgeon in Glasgow spraying carbolic acid, 1865) → antiseptic surgery
31. the spray → (it hangs as a mist that becomes the soap foam of today's hand) → soap
32. ★ the foam → (the bubbles are worlds; each bubble shows a hospital ward where fewer die, through the years) → the lives saved
33. the bubbles → (they pop into numbers rising: life expectancy) → the century of germ theory
34. the numbers → (they become the tiles of a modern hospital) → a hospital today
35. the hospital → (a nurse at a sink, washing) → the gesture continues
36. ★ the washing hands → (they are drawn over Semmelweis's hands, the same gesture across 177 years) → the rhyme
37. the two hands → (they separate; Semmelweis's fades into the engraving of Vienna) → his memory
38. the engraving → (it becomes the door handle of the opening) → the bookend
39. the door handle → (a hand reaches for it — and stops, and goes to wash first) → the meaning
40. the sink → (water runs; the dust motes in the sunbeam glitter, now we know some are alive) → the last image
41. the sunbeam → (it fades to the paper) → end
