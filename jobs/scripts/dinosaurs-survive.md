# Director's script — Nếu khủng long còn sống (dinosaurs-survive)

Written FIRST (owner, 4 Nov 2026). House look: studio/EPISODE.md §00 (2D only since 5 Nov 2026, IF CREW v3 + comic layer,
CONTENT FIRST). One vertical 9:16 film, about 6.2 min (≈ 875 words), af_heart at --speed 1.0, each beat spoken as ONE
utterance (flow); af_nicole reads no quotations here (none in this episode); am_santa not needed. Theme: museum.
Places are soft (depth of field) behind the subjects; subjects in the light with a contact shadow.

REAL: Chicxulub, Yucatan, Archaeopteryx, Liaoning, Sinosauropteryx, Microraptor, Anchiornis, Triassic, Cretaceous, Palaeogene, Mexico, Germany, China, Earth

## Spine
- Hook: one rock, 10 kilometres wide, 66 million years ago → dinosaurs ruled 165 million years, humans only 300,000 →
  two surprises (birds are dinosaurs, mammals hid 100 million years) → the promise: how long they ruled, what the
  asteroid did, why mammals stayed small, what a world without that rock would look like.
- 1) The rulers: first dinosaurs 230 million years ago → 165 million years of rule → 500 times longer than us →
  the asteroid at Chicxulub → the crater → the extinction.
- 2) The shadows: mammals nocturnal and rat-sized → the day/night deal → the asteroid misses → time passes, mammals
  still small → generations waiting.
- 3) The survivors: birds are the only surviving lineage → Archaeopteryx 1861 → Liaoning feathers → three names →
  the chicken is a dinosaur.
- 4) What people say: live side by side, keep one as a pet → the city was not built for them → what is known:
  bear-sized mammals within 10 million years, early primates soon after → growth only because the top was empty →
  the asteroid cleared the room.
- Conclusion: the asteroid cleared the top · the mammals grew into it · the birds never left → without that rock,
  no us → closing thought: the chicken is still here.

## Transitions (planned, each carries the idea): zoomthru into the asteroid and into the what-if / match the asteroid
onto the crater and the bear onto the empty throne / morph the dinosaur into the bird and the outro into one word /
push2 and wipe to move along the argument / dissolve when time passes / fade only into chapter cards.
