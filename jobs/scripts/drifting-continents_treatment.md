# Treatment — Các lục địa đang trôi (drifting-continents) · director: Claude · 17 Nov 2026
(CINEMA.md §1; the chain: jobs/scripts/drifting-continents_chain.md; research: jobs/research/drifting-continents.md)

## 1. Logline
A meteorologist noticed the world map was a torn page, was laughed at for fifty years and died on the Greenland ice — until stripes of magnetism on the ocean floor, recorded like a tape, proved the ground beneath us is moving.

## 1b. The opening (three candidates, STORY §1b patterns — keep one)
- A: Familiar made strange: a child's jigsaw — two pieces that fit are South America and Africa.
- B: The moment before: Wegener with scissors and an atlas, 1911.
- C: The impossible number: the Atlantic is wider today than yesterday — by about a fingernail a year.
- chosen, and why: Familiar made strange (A).

## 2. The feeling
- word: the ground moving under you
- the moment it lands: L35, the Atlantic opening a nail's width

## 3. Governing image & motif
- image: the torn atlas page
- motif (shape): the TEAR / SEAM: torn paper, the quilt's stitches, the mid-ocean ridge, the jigsaw edge
- returns (≥ 4): L2 — the atlas tears · L9 — Pangaea's seams · L22 — the ridge's mirror · L37 — the future jigsaw · L39 — the torn atlas again

## 4. Visual metaphors
- motion too slow to see: (1) a rock moving a fingernail while a city is built → (2) the sea floor as a tape recorder → (3) mountains as wrinkles in a rug

## 5. Set pieces
### Set piece 1 — The torn map (act I)
- idea: the continents fit — someone must have torn them
- staging: an atlas on a desk in 1911
- camera: insert on the coastlines → pull back as the page tears
- transformation: the jigsaw becomes the atlas; Wegener's scissors join the pieces
- sound moment: paper tearing, then a click
- reveal: Pangaea — once one supercontinent
### Set piece 2 — The man on the ice (act II)
- idea: the idea outlives its author
- staging: a tent in a Greenland blizzard
- camera: slow push to the lamp
- transformation: snow fills the tent; the snowfield becomes the white sea floor of a chart
- sound moment: wind, the lamp hissing out
- reveal: the proof lies under the sea
### Set piece 3 — The tape under the sea (act III)
- idea: the ocean floor recorded its own birth
- staging: a ship towing a magnetometer; the ridge
- camera: detail on the pen, then a vast aerial along the stripes
- transformation: the barcode folds along the ridge; it becomes a tape running both ways
- sound moment: the scratch of the pen, a low hum
- reveal: the sea floor spreads
