# Treatment — Thứ gì đã giết khủng long? (dinosaur-killer) · director: Claude · 17 Nov 2026
(CINEMA.md §1; the chain: jobs/scripts/dinosaur-killer_chain.md; research: jobs/research/dinosaur-killer.md)

## 1. Logline
A father and son measured a pinch of clay to time how fast it settled and found a metal from space; the crater it pointed to had already been found by oil prospectors — and forgotten in a company report.

## 1b. The opening (three candidates, STORY §1b patterns — keep one)
- A: The moment before: a dinosaur looks up at a new star brightening in the evening sky.
- B: Familiar made strange: a museum skeleton at night, and the question it cannot answer.
- C: The impossible number: the whole last day of a world lies in a line of clay one centimetre thick.
- chosen, and why: The moment before (A).

## 5. Set pieces
### Set piece 1 — The last evening (act I)
- idea: a world that doesn't know it's ending
- staging: a Cretaceous forest at dusk, engraved and coloured
- camera: close on the dinosaur's eye → it looks up
- transformation: the new star brightens; the picture freezes into a sheet of rock
- sound moment: birdsong of the Cretaceous, then silence
- reveal: the rock remembers
### Set piece 2 — The needle (act II)
- idea: a pinch of clay holds a metal from space
- staging: a Berkeley laboratory
- camera: insert on the needle → it swings past the scale
- transformation: the needle points through the roof to the sky
- sound moment: a rising whine, then a held breath
- reveal: an asteroid
### Set piece 3 — The forgotten ring (act III)
- idea: the crater was found and forgotten
- staging: a 1978 plane over the Yucatán; a dusty report
- camera: aerial over the coast, then a slow push to the shelf
- transformation: the half-ring completes under jungle and sea
- sound moment: the plane's drone, dust blown away
- reveal: Chicxulub
