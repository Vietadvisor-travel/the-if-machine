# Chain — Các lục địa đang trôi (drifting-continents) · CINEMA §0 · ★ = pure invention
1. a child's jigsaw puzzle on a table → (two pieces slide together with a click) → a fit
2. ★ the two pieces → (they become South America and Africa on an old atlas page) → the coastline fit
3. the atlas → (its paper tears along the Atlantic; the two halves drift a hair apart) → the question
4. the tear → (we drop through the page to the ground: a meadow, a rock) → solid ground
5. ★ the rock → (a time-lapse: grass grows, trees rise, a city is built, and the rock has moved only a fingernail) → the slowness
6. the fingernail → (it becomes a meteorologist's hand holding a weather balloon in Greenland) → Alfred Wegener
7. the balloon → (it rises and the world below unrolls as a map) → Wegener's view of the world
8. the map → (Wegener cuts the continents out with scissors and pushes them together) → Pangaea
9. ★ Pangaea → (its seams become stitches, like a quilt) → one supercontinent
10. the quilt → (on it, a little reptile walks from Brazil to South Africa without getting wet) → Mesosaurus
11. Mesosaurus → (it dives into its freshwater lake; the lake splits in two across an ocean) → fossils on both sides
12. ★ the split lake → (fern leaves rain down on India, Australia — and the white of Antarctica) → Glossopteris in the ice
13. the ice → (fossil leaves under snow, a fern in a freezer) → a warm Antarctica once
14. the fern → (it's held up in a lecture hall; laughter rises like steam) → 1912, the mocked lecture
15. ★ the laughter → (it becomes a wall of professors' faces, each saying "impossible!" in a speech bubble that hardens to stone) → rejection
16. the stone wall → (snow covers it; a tent in Greenland, 1930) → Wegener's last expedition
17. the tent → (snow fills it, the lamp goes out) → his death on the ice
18. ★ the snowfield → (it flattens into the white of the ocean floor on a nautical chart) → the unknown sea floor
19. the chart → (a ship's line drops a weight: sounding) → the deep
20. the line → (it becomes a cable towing a magnetometer behind a ship) → 1950s survey
21. ★ the magnetometer's needle → (its pen draws stripes on paper, light and dark, like a barcode) → magnetic stripes
22. the barcode → (it's laid on the map: stripes parallel to the mid-ocean ridge, the same on both sides, like a mirror) → symmetry
23. the mirror → (Vine and Matthews fold the paper along the ridge; the stripes match) → the insight
24. ★ the folded paper → (it becomes a cassette tape running out from the ridge in both directions, recording the Earth's magnetic field) → the sea floor as a tape recorder
25. the tape → (a compass needle flips north–south on it, again and again) → field reversals recorded
26. the recording → (molten rock wells up at the ridge, cools, carries the stripe away) → seafloor spreading
27. ★ the conveyor → (it runs under a sleeping Wegener's grave in the ice — and gently moves it) → the proof he never saw
28. the conveyor → (it reaches a trench where the floor dives down) → subduction
29. the trench → (earthquakes flash along it like a string of lights) → the plates' edges
30. the lights → (they outline the plates of the whole Earth on a globe) → plate tectonics
31. ★ the globe → (its plates move like ice floes; mountains crumple up where they push — the Himalaya rising like a rug wrinkle) → mountains born
32. the rug wrinkle → (a satellite above: GPS beams down to a marker bolted in rock) → GPS today
33. the marker → (a ruler beside it measures a few centimetres a year) → the speed today
34. ★ the ruler → (a hand beside it: the nail grows as fast as the Atlantic widens) → your fingernails
35. the fingernail → (the camera rises: the Atlantic opening a nail's width each year, Pangaea's tear still widening) → the world now
36. the Atlantic → (time runs forward: continents drift into a new arrangement) → the future map
37. ★ the future map → (it becomes the jigsaw on the child's table — pieces in places we've never seen) → the rhyme
38. the jigsaw → (the child's hand slides two pieces apart) → the last image
39. the hand → (the old atlas from the start lies beside it, torn) → bookend
40. the torn atlas → (it fades to paper) → end
