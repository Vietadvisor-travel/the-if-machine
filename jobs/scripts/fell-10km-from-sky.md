## The 4 tests
1. Hook: a piece of an airliner tumbling through the sky + "fell ten kilometres… and lived" — the whole story in one line.
2. Turn: she was not supposed to be on the plane; the food cart that pinned her and her low blood pressure saved her; she remembers none of it.
3. Sound-off: every line has its own picture (tail falling, name tags, 28 counter, flash, three pieces, cart, gauge, altitude counter, snow,
   villager, ward tags, desk, flashes).
4. Truth: every factual line cites 1,2 (facts table of the pack). Two lines rest on Guinness alone: McCartney presenting the medal (1985) and the
   "cat" quote (Guinness citing the New York Times) — said as her quote, not as narration.

## Withheld / payoff
"She did not know it yet, but that cart would save her life" (shot 9) → paid off by the impact and the ward; closing line echoes the opening
(the fall) with the irony that she never remembered it.
