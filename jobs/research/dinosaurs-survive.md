## Question (one line, as the viewer would ask it)

What if the dinosaurs had survived?

## Twist (the surprising answer, one or two lines)

The twist is not that you would keep a pet dinosaur. It is that you would probably not exist at all. Mammals spent about 100 million years staying small in the shadows of dinosaurs, and only the asteroid clearing the top of the food chain gave them room to grow into us.

## Notes / traps (numbers that are easy to get wrong, claims to avoid)

- Do not say all dinosaurs died. Birds survived, and they are dinosaurs. Say non-avian dinosaurs every time the extinction is described.
- Do not claim a comet for sure. The mainstream case is an asteroid at Chicxulub. A comet version exists in the literature, but it is not the consensus used here.
- Do not claim dinosaurs could be cloned from bird DNA. The pack sources say that is currently impossible after 66 million years of change.
- The 75 percent figure is about all species, not just dinosaurs, and some sources give 76 percent. Use "about 75 percent" in narration.
- The "no humans" outcome is the machine's guess, not a proven fact. Label it as a guess on screen. The verified part is that mammals stayed small while dinosaurs ruled.
- Keep pterosaurs and mosasaurs out of the dinosaur count. They lived with dinosaurs, they were not dinosaurs.
