## Key numbers (exact, with unit and source; "about" where the sources disagree)

- Departure: 17 Nov 2012 (Guardian; State Stories).
- Drift: 438 days; about 6,700 miles (Guardian); some reports give 5,500-6,700 miles, so say "about 6,700 miles" per the Guardian or "more than 5,500 miles" if a range is safer.
- Boat: 25 ft, about 7 m (Guardian).
- Fish dumped: about 500 kg (Guardian).
- Sea anchor: 50 buoys (Guardian).
- Lunar cycles counted: 15 (Guardian).
- Cordoba's death: about 4 months in per the book, about 1 month per early interviews; use "about four months in" and do not fix a day.
- Cordoba's age: 22 per the Guardian, 24 per other reports. Say "in his early twenties" or "22" with the Guardian as the source, never "24" as settled fact.
- Body kept: 6 days (Guardian).
- Landing: 30 Jan 2014 (Guardian; State Stories).
- Hospital: 11 days before doctors judged him stable enough to travel home (Guardian).
- Container ships passed: about 20 (Guardian).

## Visual notes (place, era, weather/light, clothing, objects, vehicles, what the room/landscape looked like)

- An open fibreglass skiff, no cabin, no sail, no oars, no anchor; a refrigerator-sized fibreglass icebox that they turn upside down and huddle inside; 50 buoys strung from the boat as a sea anchor.
- Load on departure: 70 gallons of gasoline, 16 gallons of water, 23 kg of sardines for bait, 700 hooks, a harpoon, three knives, three buckets, a mobile phone in a plastic bag, a GPS unit (not waterproof), a two-way radio, 91 kg of ice.
- Barehanded fishing: kneeling at the gunwale, arms in the water to the shoulders, hands a few inches apart, then smashing them shut on a fish.
- Sun, storms, rain collected in a grey five-gallon bucket; container ships crawling across the horizon, no visible crew.
- Landing: a tiny island no bigger than a football field, turquoise water, palm fronds and sharp coconut shells on the beach, a small canal, a beach house across it.
- Alvarenga on landing: hair matted upwards, beard wild, ankles swollen, wrists tiny, barely able to walk, naked, clutching a knife.

## Sensitivity (victims, families, living people, contested points) and how to tell it with respect

- Cordoba died. Tell with respect, never as a joke. His last request to his mother (quote 7) is the emotional centre of the middle of the clip; keep it verbatim and quiet.
- The cannibalism allegation (fact 12) must never be stated as fact. Do not put it on screen at all. It exists only in this pack so Claude knows what not to say.
- Note the separate matter: his former lawyer later filed a different $1 million suit about a contract, not about Cordoba. The two suits are different; do not mix them up, and neither belongs in the clip.
- The account rests mostly on Alvarenga's own words, supported by the drift model, the polygraph, the registration number and the missing-boat report. Say "he says" where the pack says so, and let the drift model carry the proof beat.
- End on survival and the 15 moons, not on the lawsuits.
