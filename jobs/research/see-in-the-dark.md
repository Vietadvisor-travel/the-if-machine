## Question (one line, as the viewer would ask it)

What if you could see in the dark?

## The answer (what the evidence actually says, one or two lines; objective, no storyline)

True night vision is not brighter eyes, it is seeing heat. The world would turn into a thermal map, and color would be gone.

## Notes / traps (numbers that are easy to get wrong, claims to avoid)
- Cat night vision still needs some light: it is NOT seeing in total darkness (F10). Say so on screen.
- "Six times more rods" is a density figure, not a total count: cats and humans have similar total rod numbers (~120 million); cats pack them denser. Do not say cats have six times as many rods in total.
- The 6.2x contrast figure is from one behavioural study (Kang & Malpeli 2009); the popular "6x better" claim traces to it.
- Pit viper detection distance is about 1 metre under ideal conditions, less in rain or humidity.
