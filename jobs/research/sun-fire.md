# Research pack — sun-fire · "What makes the Sun shine?" · FATHOM · Claude, 17 Nov 2026

## The question
The Sun has poured out light and heat for billions of years. No fire, no fuel we know could last that long. How did we find out what it burns — when nobody can go there, and its heart is hidden under 700,000 km of glowing gas?

## Facts
| # | fact | sources |
|---|---|---|
| F1 | A Sun made of coal (or any chemical fuel) would burn out in a few thousand years — far too short for the age of the Earth | S1, S2 |
| F2 | Helmholtz (1854) and Kelvin: the Sun shines because it slowly SHRINKS under its own gravity; that gives it only tens of millions of years (Kelvin's figures ≈ 20–24 million; "about 20 million" in the film) | S1, S2, S3 |
| F3 | Geologists and Darwin needed hundreds of millions of years — the clash between physics and geology | S1, S3 |
| F4 | 24 Aug 1920, Cardiff, British Association: Eddington — the contraction theory is "an unburied corpse"; Aston had just weighed helium: one helium atom weighs about 1 part in 120 (≈ 0.8 %) less than four hydrogen atoms; that missing mass, as energy (E = mc²), could power the Sun | S4, S5 |
| F5 | 1938–39: Hans Bethe worked out the nuclear reactions (CNO cycle; with Critchfield the proton–proton chain, which powers most of the Sun) — Nobel 1967 | S1, S6 |
| F6 | Proof needed a messenger from the core: neutrinos, made in fusion, pass straight through the Sun and the Earth | S7, S8 |
| F7 | 1960s–90s: Ray Davis's tank of cleaning fluid (≈ 600 tonnes of perchloroethylene) 1,478 m down the Homestake gold mine caught only about a third (sources: ~ 1/3) of the predicted neutrinos — the "solar neutrino problem" | S7, S8, S9 |
| F8 | 2001–02: the Sudbury Neutrino Observatory (heavy water, Canada) counted all three kinds: the missing neutrinos had changed type on the way. The Sun's fusion was confirmed. Nobel 2002 (Davis), 2015 (Kajita, McDonald) | S7, S8, S9 |
| F9 | Billions of solar neutrinos (≈ 65 billion per cm² per second) pass through every fingertip-sized patch of us each second, day and night (they cross the Earth) | S7, S10 |
| F10 | The Sun turns about 4 million tonnes of its mass into energy every second | S2, S11 |
| F11 | The Sun is ≈ 150 million km away; its radius ≈ 700,000 km; its core is ≈ 15 million °C ("millions of degrees") | S11, S12 |
| F12 | The Sun is ≈ 4.6 billion years old and has billions of years of hydrogen left | S11, S12 |
| F13 | John Bahcall's theory predicted the neutrino count Davis should see; the gap lasted ≈ 30 years (1968–2001) | S7, S8 |

## Clues
1. **Too long for fire** (F1). 2. **The shrinking Sun and the angry geologists** (F2, F3). 3. **The missing mass of helium** (F4, F5). 4. **Ghosts from the core** (F6–F8).
