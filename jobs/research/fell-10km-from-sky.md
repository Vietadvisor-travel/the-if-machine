## Hook line (verb first, mid-action, ≤ 20 words)

Pinned under a food cart, she falls ten kilometres from the sky and lives.

## The turn (what the viewer does not expect)

A villager who had been a medic in World War II hears her faint cries inside the wreckage, and she walks again after ten months.

## Key numbers (exact, with unit and source; "about" where the sources disagree)

- 10,160 m (33,333 ft): recorded fall height (S1, S2). Disputed by the 2009 missile theory (800 m); the black boxes confirm 10,160 m (S1).
- 28 aboard, 27 died, 1 survived (S1, S2).
- 22: her age (born 3 Jan 1950) (S2). Note: the Guinness article text says "23-year-old" once; her birth date makes her 22, and Wikipedia says 22. Use 22.
- 16:01: explosion time, 46 minutes after the Copenhagen departure (S1, S2).
- 10 months: time until she walked again (S1, S2).
- 66: age at death, 23 Dec 2016 (S1, S2).

## Quotes (exact words only, with who said them and the source; no invented dialogue)

- "I am like a cat, I have had nine lives," she told the New York Times. "But if nationalist forces in this country prevail, my heart will burst." - Vesna Vulovic, quoted in S1 (Guinness, citing the New York Times).
- No other direct quotation from Vulovic herself is verified in S1/S2. Do not invent dialogue. Honke's words are not recorded in either source; show him finding her, do not put words in his mouth.

## Visual notes (place, era, weather/light, clothing, objects, vehicles, what the room/landscape looked like)

- 1972, winter, Czechoslovakia: snow-covered forest and mountains near Srbská Kamenice, grey overcast light, deep snow.
- The DC-9 tail fuselage section: metal fuselage, a food trolley (service cart) pinning her in the tail, seats, snow pressing in through the torn metal.
- Vulovic: 22-year-old flight attendant, JAT uniform (1970s Yugoslav airline), dark hair.
- Honke: older villager, winter coat, walking through snow toward the wreckage.
- Hospital: 1972 Yugoslav hospital, white sheets, traction frame for the broken legs and spine.
- Do not stage the explosion as a fireball spectacle; the fall is a silent, snow-muted descent of the fuselage section.

## Sensitivity (victims, families, living people, contested points) and how to tell it with respect

- 27 real people died. Name no victim beyond Vulovic. The bomb was a terrorist attack; state the cause once, plainly, from the official report. Do not dwell on the bomb or the politics.
- The height is disputed (see fact 12). Say "recorded as 10,160 m". Never present it as settled.
- She was fired in the 1990s for protesting; one line at most, not a beat. End on her walking again and the record, not on her death.
