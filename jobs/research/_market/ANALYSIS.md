# Market analysis (Claude, 12 Nov 2026) — from Muse's scan (60 Facebook videos, 14 Aug–11 Nov 2026) + own stats

Limits: likes, not views (Facebook hides views without login); 60 videos found by engagement-ranked search; one creator
(Zack D. Films) is 12 of 60. Directional, not statistical proof.

## Own numbers
- Facebook: ~208 and ~249 views per episode (181/231 unique), 1–2 engagements. Instagram 2–4 views, TikTok 0, YouTube 2–8.
- The 6–7 min vertical films get almost no distribution. Retention and earnings could not be read (Business Suite login failed).

## What wins
- Niche ceiling (median likes): real-life stories 30K · history/mystery 26K · science what-if 1.6K · health 1K.
  Our niche (what-if) is the smallest; even the big What If page gets 1–6K likes on Facebook.
- Style (median likes, all niches): animation 21K (n=25) · real footage/mixed 11K (n=15) · talking head 4.3K (n=20).
  Animation wins in history (66K vs 23K) and what-if; real footage leads only in health, where doctors' talking heads dominate.
- Zack D. Films: median 95K likes vs 4.35K for everyone else; 9 of the top 15 history videos, the top health video (110K),
  4 of the top 15 stories. Formula: 3D animation, AI voice, NO on-screen text, ~40–60 s vertical, a TRUE micro-story or a
  concrete fact told as a story, opening mid-action on one concrete image:
  "Pulled the spring from his flashlight and bent it into a hook." · "You sliced Manhattan in half, you would see old trash
  and broken ship parts." · "You lay on your arm, your weight presses on your nerves…"
- 2D animation also works in the big niches: Project Nightfall 22K (health) / 11K (stories), History Made Easy 21K,
  Stickify 12K. 3D is not required.
- Length: winners are 40–90 s; a few 10–20 min history pieces exist.

## Conclusion
The vein: short (45–75 s) animated TRUE micro-stories (survival, history under your feet, strange real events, the body in
a concrete situation), opening mid-action on one concrete image, no on-screen text, curiosity or anxiety, one continuous
scene that follows the story. Our current format (6–7 min abstract what-if explainer, heavy text) is the opposite on every axis.
