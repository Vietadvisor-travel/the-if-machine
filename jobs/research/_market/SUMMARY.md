Method: 60 Facebook videos (15 per niche), posted 14 Aug-11 Nov 2026. Found by public Facebook search (engagement-ranked), read with facebook-cli (caption + full transcript). No login was used.
Views, shares and video length are not shown without a Facebook login, so ranking and medians use likes and comments as displayed. Lengths below are implied by transcript word counts at ~150 words/min, except where stated.
Health/body: median 1,000 likes. Real footage median 1,100 vs animation 350. Hooks: (1) "what happens to your body when..." (2) fear list / warning on screen (3) "what if" question. Typical length ~40 s (median transcript 97 words).
Science what-if: median 1,600 likes. Animation 1,450 vs real 1,100. Hooks: (1) the "what if" question itself (2) second-person "right now" address (3) flat shocking statement. Typical length ~2 min (median transcript 332 words).
Real-life stories: median 30,000 likes. Animation 30,000 vs real 27,000. Hooks: (1) in-medias-res action fragment (2) premise as caption/title (3) question to the viewer. Typical length ~50 s (median transcript 120 words).
History/mystery: median 26,000 likes. Animation 66,000 vs real 23,000. Hooks: (1) concrete mystery object/scene (2) second-person "you" invitation (3) date/place cold open. Typical length ~1.5 min, with several 10-20 min pieces (one summary states 17 min).
Surprising: one creator, Zack D. Films (3D animation + synthetic voice), holds 9 of the top 15 history videos, the top health video (110K likes), and 4 of the top 15 stories.
Surprising: stories is by far the biggest niche (median 30K likes); health and what-if top videos rarely pass 5K likes except Zack's 110K.
Surprising: the what-if niche is almost entirely animation; only 5 of 15 are real footage, and 4 of those are talking-head presenters.
Surprising: one what-if video (OrbitX, 1.9K likes) was deleted between discovery and reading; replaced by the next-best animation (space 385, 780 likes).
Limits: Facebook retention curves for our own two videos could not be captured (no login available for Business Suite); jobs/research/_market/img/ is empty. Own-stats earnings and demographics were also not shown by any platform.
Note: 7 of 60 videos had no transcript; their hooks come from the caption or on-screen text (marked in fb_scan.csv notes).
