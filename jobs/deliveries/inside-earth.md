- Wiechert, 1890s: an iron core like iron meteorites — S4, S5

- Shadow zone 103°–143° — S11, S12
- Lehmann, Copenhagen, Buller 1929 records; 1936 paper titled "P′"; oatmeal-box card files — S11, S12, S13, T-01

- Reframed ★★★ misses (no free version exists anywhere — Muse's notes): Kola rig photos/footage (P-03, V-03) → drawn; Lehmann's own seismograms/card file (P-02, D-05) →
  a DRAWN record "after Lehmann's Fig. 6, Buller 1929" (labelled on the paper) and drawn card boxes; her 1936 paper (D-01, copyright) → its one-character title "P′"
  as in-scene type with "the whole title of her 1936 paper", never a fake page; seismograph footage (V-01) → the drawn Wiechert-type instrument + the real Milne photo.
- Not to scale: the borehole shaft is drawn ~1.6× its 23 cm width so it can be seen; the crust band on the planet is thickened. The inner core and the Moon ARE to scale.
- The Kola target line is labelled "the base of the crust" (≈ 40 km drawn); the Moho depth varies by place.
- "Mars: its core, found by listening" — general statement (InSight); no number given.
