## Facts on screen (each with its source from the research pack)
- Scott Kelly, 340 days: the heart's left ventricle lost ~0.74 g a week despite exercise 6 days a week — F4 (UTSW, News-Medical; Circulation 2021)
- Weight halves, mass stays; jump height ∝ 1/g → 30 cm becomes 60 cm — F9 (physics)
- Apollo astronauts loped/skipped/hopped at 1/6 g — F1, F7
- Walk–run switch at the same Froude number, ~0.7× the speed at half g (~7 → ~5 km/h) — F12 (Kram 1997)
- Orbital speed ~7.9 → ~5.6 km/s at half g — F15 (√(gR), said as a calculation)
- Bone 1–1.5 % a month on long missions — F2 (NASA NTRS, Space.com)
- 9 of 17 astronauts had not recovered shin bone density a year after landing — F3 (Gabel 2022)
- Up to 20 % muscle mass on flights of 5–11 days — F6 (CSA, ASME citing NASA)
- JAXA centrifuge mice: 2/3 g kept muscle strong, 1/3 g did not fully protect — F11 (Science Advances 2026)
- Half the air weight → ~half the surface pressure ≈ 5,500 m today; water boils ~81 °C at half an atmosphere — F13
- Circular orbit speed = escape speed at half the pull: 30 vs 42 km/s → 30 — F14 (Astronomy magazine, Astrobites)
